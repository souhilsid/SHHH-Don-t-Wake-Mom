SHHH! — V10 Visual Interaction & Art Brief

Reference-only mood board for Claude. The goal is a physically manipulable household with hand-painted storybook surfaces and believable stylized fluid volumes. None of the source photos may be used as shipping assets.

Remote image thumbnails require internet access. Click the source link for full context.

01 · Hand-painted kitchen

Cohesive cabinet shapes, warm colors, soft painted gradients, in-place household props. Aim for an original SHHH! style of similar craftsmanship.

Artist / source

02 · Toon liquid surface language

Depth colors, rounded opacity, foam contours, large controlled highlights, stylized caustic cues. Adapt to bounded water streams, fill volumes and puddles.

Article / source

03 · Grasp & constrained drawer

Reach for the handle, open along physical rails, pick up the object with real hand pose, return and close the drawer.

Reference listing

04 · Real room, scattered work

Chores exist where the mess exists, rather than spawning a row of props on a task table. Tools are stored in authentic places.

Game screenshot / source

05 · Local contact-driven cleaning

Dirt progressively fades only along the actual affected surface area. No whole-plate instant cleaning on triggering a collider.

Game screenshot / source

06 · Tactile cartoon VR

Art and interaction must work together. Tools visibly grasp, move, collide, transfer contents, create splash/foam and respond to the world.

Game screenshot / source
Implementation criterion: in Unity PlayMode show a full sequence of grabbing a dirty plate from the dining table; operating a physical faucet, soap and sponge; performing localized scrubbing with a shaded spatial water volume; then fitting the plate into a real rack. This must happen inside the connected house, with plausible mass/contact/sound and no central chore-table spawning.
