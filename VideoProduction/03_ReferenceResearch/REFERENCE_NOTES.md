## Takeaways applied
1. **Short shots in montage, longer in the set-up.** References run 1.5-2.6 s per shot. SHHH! uses 3.0-3.4 s in the opening mood
   section and 1.6-2.3 s in the chore and chaos montages (33 shots in 1:43).
2. **Big chunky phrase cards over the world colour** (Cook-Out: "GET YOUR APRONS READY", "GATHER", "AND KEEP THE ORDERS COMING").
   SHHH! uses three: EVERY CHORE. / NOT ONE SOUND. / DON'T GET BLAMED. - in the brand's Luckiest Guy + Sunny Yellow on Night Navy.
3. **Logo late, then hold** (both inspected trailers put the logo in the last ~10 s with a call-out line). SHHH! ends on a 9 s logo hold.
4. **First-person hands are the hero** (Cosmonious High) - every SHHH! gameplay shot is the player's own hands in the house.
5. **Loudness**: the references sit at -14 to -18 LUFS; SHHH! masters to -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP (YouTube reference level).
