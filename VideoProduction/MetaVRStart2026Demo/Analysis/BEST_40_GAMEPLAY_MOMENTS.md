Quest captures; IDs refer to `COMPLETE_FOOTAGE_ANALYSIS.csv`. ✔ = used in the demo.

Method: full-duration contact sheets for every recording (1 frame / 3–4 s), then 1–4 fps frame strips across every interaction
(`Frames/*.jpg`: DISH, PUMP, SCRUB, CRASH, CHEST, FRIDGE, REDFRIDGE, BIN, LOBBY, MPEND, SOLOSET, TOWEL, BLANKET, PLATES06),
plus task-card crops to read what each job actually is. Audio was checked by measurement and speech recognition, not by listening.
