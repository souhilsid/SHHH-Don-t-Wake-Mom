## Trailer - title: SHHH! - Don't Wake Mom! | Official Game Trailer
It's late. The house is a mess. And Mom is finally asleep.
SHHH! - Don't Wake Mom! is a VR comedy for Meta Quest: do your chores with your own hands - pair the slippers, stack the mugs,
fold the towels, scrub the plates - without making a sound. Pets, siblings and every little clink can wake Mom... and when she
finds a mess, somebody's getting blamed.

All gameplay captured on Meta Quest from in-development builds. Narration is a synthetic voice made for this trailer.

"Sneaky Adventure", "Scheming Weasel (faster version)", "Investigations"
